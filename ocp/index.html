四大 OCP 官方认证体系导航 The 4 Official OCP Certification Pillars

OCP Accepted™

最高级别的开放性。产品完全符合 OCP 规范，且制造商需向社区完全开源其物理硬件设计文件 (Design Packages)。Highest level of openness. Complies fully with OCP specs, requiring manufacturers to open-source physical hardware Design Packages to the community.

OCP Inspired™

合规且保护产权。产品严格符合 OCP 规范，但制造商可保留专有设计图纸的知识产权，无需开源。Compliant yet IP-protected. Fully meets OCP specs, but allows manufacturers to retain proprietary design blueprints without open-sourcing.

OCP S.A.F.E.

安全评估框架。针对设备固件与安全架构，确保供应链的完整性并抵御恶意固件攻击。Security Appraisal Framework. Targets device firmware and architecture to ensure supply chain integrity against malicious firmware attacks.

OCP Ready™

设施级认证。证明数据中心机房的承重、电力密度和空间布局足以为极高密度的 OCP 液冷机架提供支撑。Facility-level certification. Proves a data center's floor loading, power density, and layout can support ultra-high-density OCP liquid-cooled racks.

从底层硅片、液冷架构到机架级供电，dcdeeptech 协助中国产业链制造商在以下 6 大核心 OCP 项目中完成工程对接与认证孵化。 From core silicon and liquid cooling to rack-level power, dcdeeptech assists manufacturers in engineering integration and certification across 6 core OCP Projects.

核心计算与服务器Compute & Server

  • 开放加速器基础设施 (OAI) 标准化 GPU/TPU 模块接口 (OAM) 与通用基板 (UBB)，打破 AI 算力物理锁定，支持单模块高达 400W 功耗。 Standardized GPU/TPU module interfaces (OAM) and Universal Baseboards (UBB), breaking AI lock-in, supporting up to 400W per module.
  • 开放小芯片经济 (Chiplet) 定义 Die-to-Die (D2D) 物理接口，推动异构硅芯片在单一系统级封装 (SiP) 中的集成。 Defining Die-to-Die (D2D) physical interfaces, driving heterogeneous silicon integration into a single System-in-Package (SiP).
  • OCP NIC 3.0 网卡规范 前置热插拔夹层设计，涵盖 SFF 到 TDSFF 外形，支持高达 400 Gb/s 的网络吞吐与局域网旁路 (Bypass)。 Front-pluggable mezzanine designs (SFF to TDSFF), supporting up to 400 Gb/s throughput and LAN Bypass.

网络解耦与横向扩展Disaggregated Networking

  • 裸金属交换机与 ONIE 将物理交换机硬件与网络操作系统 (NOS/SONiC) 剥离，通过 SAI 接口实现跨 Broadcom/Barefoot 芯片的统一编程。 Decoupling bare-metal switches from Network OS (SONiC) via SAI, allowing unified programming across Broadcom/Barefoot ASICs.
  • ESUN 1.0 (面向 AI 的以太网) 淘汰传统 IP/UDP 头部，引入紧凑的 4 字节 ESUN 头部。通过链路级优先流控，打造 AI 训练集群专用的无损网络结构。 Replacing legacy IP/UDP headers with a compact 4-byte ESUN header. Enforces link-level flow control for lossless AI training fabrics.

机架架构与高级配电Rack & Advanced Power

  • Open Rack V3 (ORv3) 打破 19 英寸限制的 21 英寸内宽设计，极大提升单水平空间的计算与散热密度。 21-inch inner-width design breaking the 19-inch legacy limit, vastly improving compute and thermal density per horizontal U.
  • 48V 直流母线配电架构 取代传统 12V 架构，以 48V 直流母线排 (Busbar) 将线路热损耗降低 16 倍，支撑单机柜 100kW 以上超高功率。 Replacing 12V with 48V DC busbars to reduce I²R loss by 16x, supporting extreme densities exceeding 100kW per rack.
  • 机架级电池备份 (BBU) 将备用电池直接挂载机架母线，消除数据中心对庞大集中式 UPS 的依赖。 Mounting Backup Battery Units directly to the rack busbar, eliminating DC reliance on massive centralized UPS rooms.

高级热管理与液冷Thermal & Liquid Cooling

  • 冷板技术 (Cold Plate) & CDU 直接到芯片 (Direct-to-Chip) 的微通道散热与防漏盲插连接器。冷却液分配单元 (CDU) 提供精准流量调节。 Direct-to-Chip micro-channel cooling and leak-proof blind-mate connectors. Coolant Distribution Units (CDU) for precise flow regulation.
  • 浸没式冷却 (Immersion) 定义绝缘介电液体化学兼容性、水箱结构完整性及冷却液生命周期管理。 Defining dielectric fluid chemical compatibility, tank structural integrity, and fluid lifecycle management.
  • 余热回收 (Heat Reuse) 捕获数据中心庞大热能，引流至区域城市供暖或大型温室农业。 Capturing massive thermal energy and directing it to district heating or large-scale greenhouse agriculture.

设施与边缘拓扑Facilities & Edge Topology

  • 模块化数据中心 (MDC) 预制化、集装箱化单元规范，允许在偏远地区进行极速扩展部署。 Prefabricated, containerized unit specifications enabling rapid expansion deployments in remote locations.
  • openEDGE 与 DCSG 定义短深度机箱以适应通信塔基站严苛环境；解耦蜂窝基站网关 (DCSG) 聚合 5G 流量。 Short-depth chassis for harsh cell-tower environments; Disaggregated Cell Site Gateways (DCSG) to aggregate 5G traffic.
  • EDSFF 高密度存储 (Storage) 推广 E1.S / E3.S 等面向网络组合的 NVMe 独立资源池规范。 Promoting E1.S / E3.S network-composable NVMe independent resource pool specifications.

硬件管理与可信安全Mgmt & Trusted Security

  • DC-SCM 安全控制模块 物理剥离 BMC、闪存与主板，使得 CPU 代际升级时可保留并重用安全管理固件体系。 Physically decouples BMC & Flash from mainboards, allowing reuse of secure management firmware across CPU generations.
  • 硬件信任根 (Root of Trust) 平台主动信任根 (PA RoT) 结合外设证明，从硬件底层防范 Bootkit 等固件级攻击。 Platform Active RoT (PA RoT) combined with peripheral attestation to prevent Bootkit firmware attacks at the hardware level.
  • 开放平台固件 (OSF) & 时间卡 推广 Coreboot/LinuxBoot 开源堆栈；集成高精度时间卡 (TAP)，实现纳秒级 PTP 同步以太网。 Promoting Coreboot/LinuxBoot stacks; Integrating precision Time Cards (TAP) for nanosecond PTP atomic ethernet synchronization.
