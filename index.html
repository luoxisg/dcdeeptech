1. Cooling loop – from cooling tower to rack CDU

A modern liquid-cooled AI rack is part of a three-stage cooling chain: cooling tower and condenser water, chiller and facility water system (FWS), and the technology cooling system (TCS) that serves CDUs and racks.

Cooling tower and chiller provide cold water to the Facility Water System (FWS). CDUs couple FWS to the rack coolant loop.

Key design points

  • Clearly separate CWS / FWS / TCS to simplify ownership.
  • Size CDUs for realistic IT load, not just nameplate power.
  • Plan isolation valves and bypass paths for safe maintenance.
  • Instrument temperature, pressure and flow at CDU inlets and outlets for predictive maintenance.

For immersion, we work with OCP-inspired dielectric fluids suitable for multi-year operation.

Immersion-specific considerations

  • Evaluate fluid lifetime, compatibility with plastics/seals.
  • Design tanks and service tools for safe lifting of heavy servers.
  • Plan fluid monitoring (moisture, particles, acidity).

2. Liquid cooling options

At 100kW+ per rack, air is not enough. We evaluate three topologies.

Direct-to-Chip (DLC)

Coolant flows through cold plates on CPUs/GPUs. Most precise. Requires robust manifolds.

RDHx

Liquid-cooled radiator on the rear door. Ideal for upgrading existing rooms without touching servers.

Immersion

Submerged servers in dielectric fluid. High efficiency but complex service model.

CDU

The "heart" connecting facility water to rack coolant. Controls temp, pressure and flow.

6. Technical FAQ

Comprehensive answers for OCP deployment.

Efficiency and physics. Delivering 100kW at 48V requires ~2083 Amps. According to Joule's Law ($P=I^2R$), this creates massive heat loss. Moving to 800V reduces current to ~125 Amps (a 94% reduction), slashing resistive losses by >99% and enabling thinner, cheaper cables.

Liquid is heavy. A fully populated rack handles static fluid weight plus dynamic stress from blind-mate connectors. ORV3 specs require "Web Stiffeners" on vertical posts and reinforced welding to prevent frame warping.

Megawatt-class racks weigh 3500kg+. Traditional casters will fail. ORW racks use "Support Bases" and require "Lift-and-Place" logistics using industrial gantries via top lift points.

To ensure grounding integrity in non-sterile DC environments. ASTMB117 ensures grounding points don't corrode over 10 years, preventing fatal safety failures during HVDC short circuits.

冷卻塔與冰水機提供冷卻水給建築 FWS。CDU 將 FWS 與機櫃冷卻迴路耦合。

設計重點

  • CDU 容量應以實際 IT 負載為準。
  • 預留隔離閥與旁路方便檢修。
  • 在進出口設置溫度、壓力與流量監測。

OCP Inspired 介電液，具備高閃點與嚴格潔淨度管控。

  • 監測含水量與微粒。

6. 技術 FAQ

常見技術問題詳解。

為了效率。在 48V 下傳輸 100kW 需要 2083A 電流，熱損驚人。升級到 800V 將電流降至 125A（減少 94%），使線損降低 99% 以上。

液體很重。機櫃需承受靜態重量及盲插接頭的推力。OCP 規範要求垂直立柱增加「腹板加強筋」以防變形。

兆瓦級機櫃重達 3500kg。需使用「支撐底座」分散負載，並透過頂部吊點進行工業級「吊裝定位」。

確保接地完整性。ASTMB117 測試確保接地點在 10 年內不腐蝕，防止 HVDC 系統發生安全事故。

OCP 機櫃寬 21 英寸。適配器允許在其中安裝標準 19 英寸設備，實現舊機房的漸進式升級。

消除「線纜亂麻」，改善氣流，並透過盲插減少物理連接點，提高可靠性。

標準 1U 為 44.45mm。ORV3 增加到 48mm，為高效散熱器和風扇提供關鍵的垂直空間。

在 800V 高壓下，微小的錫鬚可能引發災難性電弧。OCP 強制要求使用鍍銀或合金來防止此問題。
