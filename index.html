1. Cooling loop – from cooling tower to rack CDU

A modern liquid-cooled AI rack is part of a three-stage cooling chain: cooling tower and condenser water, chiller and facility water system (FWS), and the technology cooling system (TCS) that serves CDUs and racks.

Cooling tower/Chiller (FWS) couples with Rack Loop (TCS) via CDU.

Key design points

  • Clearly separate CWS / FWS / TCS to simplify ownership and maintenance.
  • Size CDUs for realistic IT load, not just nameplate power.
  • Plan isolation valves and bypass paths for safe maintenance.
  • Instrument temperature, pressure and flow at CDU inlets and outlets for predictive maintenance.

OCP-inspired dielectric fluids for immersion.

Immersion-specific considerations

  • Evaluate fluid lifetime, compatibility with plastics and seals, and vendor support model.
  • Design tanks, racks and service tools for safe lifting and draining of heavy servers.
  • Plan fluid monitoring (moisture, particles, acidity) as part of the maintenance workflow.

2. Liquid cooling options

At 100 kW+ per rack, air alone is no longer enough. We evaluate three rack-level topologies driven by a CDU.

Direct-to-Chip (DLC)

Coolant flows through cold plates mounted directly on CPUs/GPUs. Most precise heat removal. Requires robust manifolds, QDs, and reinforced rack frames.

RDHx

Rear Door Heat Exchanger. A radiator on the rear door. Fans push hot air through it. Ideal for upgrading existing rooms without touching server internals.

Immersion

Entire servers submerged in dielectric fluid. Single-phase or two-phase (boiling). Powerful, but changes service model and supply chain.

The CDU

The "heart". Pump and heat exchanger station connecting facility water to rack coolant. Controls temp/pressure and provides sensor data.

Export finance advisory – “EFS–LIS–SBLC” model

For Brazil and other export projects, our partner AxiomDeta can also advise on trade finance structure, avoiding rigid, high-risk back-to-back letters of credit.

The recommended structure is an “EFS–LIS–SBLC” combination for 90-day payment terms: AxiomDeta applies to a Singapore bank with a strong ESG trade track record for a revolving Enterprise Financing Scheme – Trade Loan (EFS-TL) facility. Approval and pricing are anchored by a Loan Insurance Scheme (LIS) that covers the Brazilian buyer’s credit risk.

A portion of the EFS-TL headroom is then used to issue a Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) in favour of the Shenzhen factory as a payment guarantee, separating financing (EFS-TL) from supplier security (SBLC).

This structure provides a flexible working-capital pool to pay the factory and allows repayment whenever collections are received, minimising interest cost.

1. 冷卻環路：從冷卻塔到機櫃 CDU

現代液冷 AI 機櫃通常位於三段式冷卻鏈的末端： 冷卻塔與冷凝水系統 (CWS)、 冰水機與建築供水系統 (FWS)， 以及服務 CDU 與機櫃的 設備冷卻系統 (TCS)。

FWS 與 TCS 透過 CDU 進行熱交換。

設計重點

  • 清楚區分 CWS / FWS / TCS，讓權責與維護邊界乾淨。
  • CDU 容量應以實際 IT 負載與冗餘需求為依據，而非僅看銘牌功率。
  • 預留隔離閥與旁路，方便檢修而不影響整個機房。
  • 在 CDU 進出口布置溫度、壓力與流量感測器，支援預測性維護。

OCP Inspired 介電浸沒液。

浸沒式系統額外考量

  • 評估工作液壽命、與塑膠件與密封件的相容性，以及原廠技術支援模式。
  • 機槽與機架需支援重型伺服器的吊掛、抽換與排液作業。
  • 將含水量、微粒與酸值等液體監測納入例行維護流程。

2. 液冷拓樸：DLC、RDHx、浸沒式與 CDU

當單櫃功率來到 100 kW 等級時，僅靠風冷已無法有效帶走熱量。我們評估三種主要拓樸。

芯片直冷 (DLC)

冷卻液透過冷板直接流經 CPU/GPU。效率最高，但需要穩健的歧管設計、快速接頭以及加強的機櫃結構。

後門熱交換器 (RDHx)

在機櫃後門安裝大型液冷散熱器。適合在不改動伺服器內部的情況下，升級既有機房的散熱能力。

浸沒式液冷

將整台伺服器完全浸泡在介電液中。單相或兩相。熱性能優異，但維護模式與供應鏈需重新規劃。

CDU

液冷系統的「心臟」。提供循環動力，同時控制溫度、壓力與流量，也是監控數據的來源。

6. 技術 FAQ

核心原因是功率密度和效率。 100kW 機櫃在 48V 下電流高達 2083A，導致災難性熱損耗 (I²R) 並需要昂貴的銅導體。轉向 800V 後電流降至 125A (降低 94%)，線損降低超過 99.7%，並允許使用更細的線纜。

液冷引入了 靜態重量（液體重）和 動態應力（盲插快換接頭的推拉力）。ORV3 規範要求對垂直立柱增加 “腹板加強筋” (Web Stiffeners) 以防止框架變形。

滿載 ORW 機櫃可達 3500 公斤。解決方案包括：1) 工業物流：使用頂部吊點 (Top Lift Points) 進行起重機作業。2) 負載分散：使用支撐底座取代腳輪，防止壓壞高架地板。

確保長期接地完整性。腐蝕會導致接地電阻增加，在 HVDC 短路等故障發生時帶來致命安全風險。OCP 強制要求通過 ASTMB117 測試。

ORV3 為 21 英寸寬，傳統設備為 19 英寸。此適配器允許在現代 ORV3 機櫃中安裝舊有 19 英寸設備，是棕地 (Brownfield) 部署和過渡的關鍵橋樑。
