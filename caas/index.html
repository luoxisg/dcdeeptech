Executive Summary执行摘要

Commissioned by dcdeeptech, this report outlines the deployment strategy for "C-robot" in Singapore. As a Shenzhen Government recommended product, C-robot has secured strategic support within the JTC ecosystem. 本深度研究报告由新加坡 dcdeeptech 公司委托，旨在全面评估并规划“C-robot无人清洁机器人”在新加坡市场的落地策略。作为深圳市政府重点推荐产品，C-robot已获得与新加坡裕廊集团(JTC)的战略支持。

Core Opportunity:核心机遇： Singapore faces a dual squeeze from labor costs (PWM driving >10% CAGR) and digital transformation. JTC's "Outcome-Based Contracts" (OBC) provide the institutional guarantee for automation. 新加坡清洁行业面临劳动力成本（PWM政策导致CAGR > 10%）激增与数字化转型的双重压力。JTC推行的“以结果为导向的合同”(OBC)为自动化提供了制度保障。

Business Value:商业价值： Leveraging Shenzhen supply chain to fill the "Outdoor/Semi-outdoor" gap. MBA-level models validate that CaaS can save FM providers 35%-45% in OPEX and deliver 12%-15% IRR to Capital firms. C-robot凭借深圳供应链优势填补“室外/半室外”市场空白。MBA级模型验证显示，CaaS模式可帮助FM服务商节约 35%-45% 运营成本，并为资本方提供 12%-15% 的IRR。

Strategic Context: SCI Dual Empowerment战略背景：SCI双边赋能

  • Policy Trust政策互信 Shenzhen Gov recommendation enables mutual recognition of standards and data compliance fast-track. 深圳市政府推荐，技术标准互认与数据合规快车道。
  • Complementary Strengths优势互补 Shenzhen's Hardware/AI + Singapore's High-Value Standardized Scenarios (JTC). 深圳的硬件供应链/AI算法 + 新加坡的高价值规范化场景(JTC)。
  • Solving JTC Pain PointsJTC痛点解决 Integration into ODP (Open Digital Platform), filling the gap in outdoor automation. 集成至ODP开放数字平台，填补室外自动化空白。

Mitigation: Develop "Rain Mode" algorithm; IP65 waterproofing. 缓解：开发“雨天模式”算法，硬件IP65级防水加强。
