OUR PERSPECTIVE

Community Living Memories

Oral history digitization can help families retain an elder’s voice, language and personal perspective. A consent-led programme explains the purpose, recording method and future audience before participation, and treats private family access separately from public storytelling.

Community heritage work should allow participants to decline, pause or restrict material. Family permission alone may not cover everyone mentioned in an interview. Safeguarding, retention and withdrawal arrangements must be agreed for each project. DeepFamily does not claim medical, therapeutic or guaranteed wellbeing outcomes.

COMMUNITY LIVING MEMORIES · PROPOSED PILOT

One Family's Support. Five to Ten Families' Living Memories.

DeepFamily proposes an organisation-led community heritage initiative where a sponsoring family or family office supports a partner organisation in coordinating private digital memories for five to ten participating households.

Programme concept only · Institutional partners, funding, dates and beneficiary households are not confirmed.

5–10HOUSEHOLDS · PROPOSED

Community partner coordinates the cohort. A sponsoring family may fund it.

◆Community organisations only. We do not accept applications, self-referrals, nominations or personal records from individuals or households.

HOW THE COMMUNITY PROGRAMME WORKS

A transparent community-led process.

  1. 01

    Institutional discussion

    Confirm the organisation's authority, purpose and designated representative.

  2. 02

    Written scope & funding

    Set the programme scope, roles, safeguards, financial arrangements and reporting in writing.

  3. 03

    Partner-managed invitations

    The community partner identifies and contacts 5–10 households through its own appropriate and confidential process.

  4. 04

    Voluntary interviews & archives

    Record stories and selected documents only after informed permissions and privacy review.

  5. 05

    Private delivery & reporting

    Give each participating family access-controlled digital memories and portable export; provide only aggregated sponsor reporting.

WHAT PARTICIPATING HOUSEHOLDS MAY RECEIVE

Private digital memories, owned by the participating families.

  • 01Voluntary elder or family oral-history interviews
  • 02Selected photographs and historical documents digitised
  • 03A short private family-memory documentary
  • 04A private, access-controlled online family memory collection
  • 05A portable digital archive with agreed hosting and transfer terms

Actual quantities, schedules and deliverables are defined in an institutional agreement. This initiative is distinct from the S$98,000 private family heritage service.

FILM · COMING AFTER APPROVAL

STEWARDSHIP & PRIVACY

Dignity, Consent & Community Accountability

Participation must be voluntary. Sponsorship does not give the donor access to family names, interviews or private archives. Any public story requires separate publication consent. The initiative is cultural, not clinical, and does not promise wellbeing outcomes.
