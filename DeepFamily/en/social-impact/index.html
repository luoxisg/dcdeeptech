OUR PERSPECTIVE

Community Living Memories

DeepFamily proposes a community-partner-led model in which one sponsor family supports five local households. The included household service is deliberately limited to self-uploaded family materials, two complimentary Blu-ray discs per household and cloud storage under agreed terms. The public website does not accept uploads or individual applications.

Community participation should be voluntary, with suitable permissions for the material each household chooses to share. Access, retention and withdrawal arrangements must be agreed before any service opens. The sponsored household scope does not include field collection, bespoke historical research or custom documentary production.

COMMUNITY LIVING MEMORIES · PROPOSED PROGRAMME

One Family's Support. Five Local Families' Memories.

DeepFamily is developing a family-supported community heritage initiative. Each participating sponsor family would support five local households through an eligible community organisation that coordinates participation.

Proposed programme · Partner organisations, funding, access arrangements and dates remain to be confirmed.

5LOCAL HOUSEHOLDS · PROPOSED

One sponsor family supports five households coordinated by a community partner.

HOW THE PROPOSED PARTNERSHIP WORKS

A transparent community-led process.

  1. 01

    Organisational discussion

    An eligible organisation and sponsor family discuss a five-household cohort without sharing beneficiary records through this website.

  2. 02

    Written programme scope

    Agree eligibility, self-upload access, two complimentary Blu-ray discs per household, cloud storage, responsibilities and funding in writing.

  3. 03

    Partner-managed invitations

    The organisation contacts and coordinates five local households through its authorised process. There is no direct household application here.

  4. 04

    Voluntary participation

    Each household decides what to share and receives suitable notice, permissions and access to the agreed upload service when it is available.

  5. 05

    Limited digital delivery

    Each participating household self-uploads its materials and receives two complimentary Blu-ray discs and cloud storage; bespoke research and film production are not included.

  6. 06

    Accountable reporting

    The organisation and sponsor receive an aggregate programme update without automatic access to household files or identities.

THE INCLUDED COMMUNITY SCOPE

Private digital memories, owned by the participating families.

  • 01Family materials uploaded by the participating household itself
  • 02Two complimentary Blu-ray discs per household
  • 03Cloud storage under agreed access and retention terms

This limited support is separate from the sponsor family's own private heritage project. Field collection, historical research and custom films are outside the included five-household scope unless separately agreed.

STEWARDSHIP & PRIVACY

Dignity, Choice & Community Accountability

Participation is voluntary. Sponsorship does not give a family office or donor access to household names or private files. Public stories require separate permission. This is a cultural heritage initiative, not a clinical service. It is inclusive across Singapore's communities, cultures and languages without requiring membership of any particular ethnicity, religion or clan.
