Our Privacy Principles

Heritage records may include living people’s personal data, multigenerational relationships, historical genealogy and archives, oral interviews, photographs, video, AI-assisted narratives and sensitive information. Families guide publication permissions; a family leader’s agreement does not automatically constitute consent from every living member.

Controls currently verifiable on this website

  • There is no family-record upload or account portal on this website.
  • Public case content is anonymized and clearly marked where illustrative or uncommissioned.
  • Video publication is disabled until asset rights and publication approval are cleared.

Proposed project controls requiring implementation

  • Identity-based access, encryption, access logging, backups and vendor review.
  • Processes for withdrawal, correction, deletion, retention limits and disputed records.
  • Accurate disclosure of cross-border remote access and hosting locations.
EDUCATIONAL REGULATORY OVERVIEW

Four-Country Regulatory Requirements

Singapore — PDPA

Personal Data Protection Act 2012

Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)

Potentially relevant requirements

Consider lawful collection, notification and consent (including applicable exceptions), protection, access and correction, retention limitation, breach notification, DPO/accountability duties and comparable protection for overseas transfers.

Cross-border processing

Cross-border transfers must meet the Act’s protection requirements; consent alone may not resolve every obligation.

Family archive implications

For family archives, document purpose and source, restrict access to authorized family members, and set retention and incident procedures.

Malaysia — PDPA

Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and 2024 amendments

Personal Data Protection Commissioner

Potentially relevant requirements

Review the amended framework and phased 2025 requirements relevant to DPO appointment, breach notification, data portability and cross-border transfers. Confirm current 2026 registration and sector requirements.

Cross-border processing

Assess the statutory transfer conditions and the receiving jurisdiction; do not assume local storage is universally required.

Family archive implications

Plan transparent notices, access/correction processes, vendor roles, retention and response procedures for family and oral-history records.

Official government source ↗

Indonesia — UU PDP

Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection

Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs legal information

Potentially relevant requirements

Assess lawful processing grounds, rights of data subjects, specified sensitive categories, controller and processor responsibilities, and breach notification obligations.

Cross-border processing

International transfers are subject to statutory conditions, including protection-equivalence and other applicable mechanisms.

Family archive implications

Identify sensitive family details, establish controller/processor responsibilities, and create a documented rights and incident process.

Official government source ↗

Thailand — PDPA

Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019)

Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee

Potentially relevant requirements

Assess lawful bases, consent, sensitive personal data, data-subject rights, DPO obligations where applicable and breach notification duties.

Cross-border processing

Apply safeguards and conditions under Sections 28 and 29 and relevant subordinate rules. See the government cross-border reference.

Family archive implications

Review each participant’s consent and lawful basis, access roles, interview recordings, and safeguards before any overseas access.

Cross-Border Heritage Data Governance
跨境家族档案数据治理

Consider a hypothetical historical Chinese family with branches in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand. Sources, family members, researchers and service providers may involve several jurisdictions at once.

  1. Collect records and verify sources and purpose.
  2. Record member consent and any other applicable lawful processing grounds.
  3. Apply local access roles and publication permissions.
  4. Document processors, vendor terms and locations.
  5. Disclose overseas R&D access, including China-based teams, after arrangements are confirmed.
  6. Design encryption, access logging and security incident processes.
  7. Agree family-controlled export, retention and deletion arrangements.
