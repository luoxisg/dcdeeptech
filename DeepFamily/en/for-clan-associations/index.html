OUR PERSPECTIVE

For Clan Associations

Chinese clan heritage in Singapore connects family registers, association histories and the wider Chinese diaspora. Clan archive digitization can begin with a source inventory, rights review and consistent image and transcription records. Living-person data and private member-family material need specific permission controls.

Collaboration can take the form of a cultural programme or separately scoped member-family research. Member-family participation and funding remain voluntary; the association is not assumed to pay. No named clan association is presented as a customer, commissioning party or endorser without documented approval.
