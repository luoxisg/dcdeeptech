OUR PERSPECTIVE

For Family Offices

The high-net-worth family owns the direction of its private heritage project. A family office can coordinate stakeholder interviews, approvals, budgets, archival access and succession of responsibilities under a written mandate. The S$98,000 Singapore standard project includes the family's own work and a separate S$25,000 community component for five local households; the latter is not an additional charge.

A family council may review shared cultural priorities, while a clan association participates only where records or activities concern its own remit and permissions. The family office does not automatically see member-family or beneficiary files. DeepFamily's role is cultural and historical, not regulated financial, investment, trust, tax or legal advice.

A FAMILY-LED PARTNERSHIP FRAMEWORK

One family legacy, three clear relationships.

A high-net-worth family begins with its own private heritage project. A family office can coordinate governance; a family council or clan association can help protect shared history; a qualified community organisation can deliver the proposed five-household support. Each relationship needs its own scope, consent and decision rights.

01

The family & family office

The family chooses the stories, participants and access rules. Its family office may coordinate a private proposal, approvals, budget and continuity plan, without turning heritage work into financial, trust, tax or legal advice.

Family-office approach ↗
02

Family council & clan association

A family council or clan association may convene elders, catalogue authorised records and agree cultural activities. Member-family projects remain voluntary and separately scoped; the association is not presumed to fund or endorse them.

Association approach ↗
03

Community heritage support

Through an eligible community organisation, one sponsor family may support five local households. The partner manages invitations; households choose what to share. Sponsors receive aggregate progress, not automatic access to names or private files.

Community approach ↗

This is a proposed collaboration framework, not a confirmed sponsor, association commission, charitable campaign or public household application. Agreements, safeguarding and permissions must precede delivery.
