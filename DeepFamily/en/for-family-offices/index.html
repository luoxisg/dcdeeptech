OUR PERSPECTIVE

For Family Offices

For family offices and family governance advisers, non-financial family wealth preservation concerns memory, values and context. Founder interviews and documented family history can help later generations understand decisions, alongside a private archive with agreed access and continuity arrangements.

Family office legacy planning should define who participates, who controls publication and how conflicting recollections are preserved. DeepFamily’s role is cultural and historical. It does not provide regulated financial, investment, trust, tax or legal advice, and a heritage project should not be represented as a substitute for those advisers.
