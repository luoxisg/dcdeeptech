Section 03 · Integrity, Open Formats & Strict AI Boundaries

Signed Fixity Chains, Open Preservation Formats & AI/LLM Isolation

The Real 50-Year Threat: Intact Bytes, Obsolete Software

Even if every byte survives to 2077, proprietary software extinction turns archives into unreadable noise. Aligned with the Library of Congress 2025–2026 Recommended Formats Statement and Heritage Data Protocol (HDP v1.0), DeepFamily enforces a dual-track policy (Original Master + Open Preservation Format) and decrees that LLMs and vector databases are strictly rebuildable derivatives—never primary preservation masters.

Manuscripts & Clan Books Original scan + PDF/A archival standard + TIFF master where needed
Elder Oral Histories Original recording + uncompressed WAV / BWF broadcast master

Cryptographic Chains & AI/LLM Non-Mutation Rules

  • Hash + Digital Signature + Independent Log: Storing raw SHA-256 hashes alone fails if an attacker alters both data and manifest. Manifests are digitally signed and written to an append-only log in an independent domain, supporting smooth cryptographic upgrades while retaining legacy proof chains.
  • Beyond GEDCOM: Because GEDCOM loses nuanced Chinese clan semantics and custom fields, every export bundles structured JSON/CSV, schema documentation, and an explicit media-binding manifest.
  • AI/LLM as Rebuildable Derivative: In 2057 or 2077, superior models can re-index embeddings or re-render avatars from pristine masters. Vector stores must never replace primary text, audio, and provenance records.
  • Four-State Fact Governance: Conflicting oral histories retain explicit Confirmed / Pending / Family Testimony / AI Inferred states (72-point confidence threshold). AI never adjudicates historical truth for a family.
