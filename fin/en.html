Section 01 · Executive Summary

Category-Defining Infrastructure for Asia's Great Wealth & Memory Transfer

Investment Thesis

DeepFamily is not a consumer genealogy app or a bespoke memoir agency. We are building the sovereign digital asset vault and intergenerational AI infrastructure for UHNW Chinese families in Singapore and Southeast Asia. By pairing high-margin, upfront-paid white-glove onboarding (0-month CAC payback) with our HDP v1.0 data governance protocol, air-gapped Blu-ray custody, and a WhatsApp/WeChat-native AI steward, we capture an irreplaceable private knowledge graph that compounds in stickiness across decades.

Southeast Asia's 1st- and 2nd-generation Chinese business patriarchs are aged 65–85. Their dialect oral histories, decision heuristics, and family provenance face permanent loss within 5–10 years. Demand is driven by urgency, not discretionary software upgrades.

Hardware costs have been compressed to S$7,600 (Mac mini M5 Pro + 5× 18TB Blu-ray). With 53.8% upfront payment covering hardware and ingestion, DeepFamily achieves 0-month CAC payback, 56.2% net gross margin (after channel fees), and breaks even at just 4.33 families/year.

Where consumer AI legacy startups (e.g., HereAfter AI) collapsed due to cloud-only existential trust failure, DeepFamily leads with PDPA 2012 compliance, OAIS open-format anti-lock-in, and WORM Blu-ray physical sovereignty—winning direct credibility with SFOs, private banks, and legal advisors.

Section 04 · Core Technology Moat

Our proprietary DeepFamily Heritage Data Protocol v1.0 (DF-HDP-STD-001) is a five-layer archival and governance architecture benchmarked against international museum/archival standards (ISO 14721:2025 OAIS, W3C PROV-O, PREMIS 3.0, IIIF 3.0) and Singapore's PDPA 2012.

Figure 3: HDP v1.0 five-layer architecture. Angel proceeds (39% R&D allocation) harden this specification into automated server-side schema enforcement, RGT gates, and one-click OAIS archival packaging.

Anti-Hallucination Trust Engine

Every family fact carries an explicit epistemic status. AI extractions below the 72-point confidence threshold are automatically routed to human review. Deterministic kinship math runs in server-side code—never by LLM guesswork—eliminating "fabricated ancestors."

Chinese & SEA Dialect Semantics

Handles cross-script identity resolution (e.g., Traditional Chinese clan registers vs. Hokkien/Teochew romanizations like Tan/Lim/Teo vs. colonial land deeds), rare CJK/IVS characters, lunar/reign-era dates (date_raw + date_precision), and complex adoption/dual-heir lineages.

Anti-Lock-In = Ultimate Trust

UHNW patriarchs fear vendor bankruptcy. By guaranteeing open-standard exports plus a zero-dependency offline HTML browser burned onto every archival Blu-ray, families know their heritage remains readable 50+ years from now—even without DeepFamily's servers.

Section 05 · Regulatory & Legal Moat

Institutional-Grade Singapore PDPA Compliance Built Into Code

Private family archives sit at the intersection of Singapore's PDPA 2012 (2020 Amendment), Copyright Act 2021 (including Part 7 Moral Rights), Defamation Act 1957, and Computer Misuse Act 1993. DeepFamily's legal-engineering architecture turns regulatory friction into a decisive entry barrier against generic consumer apps and offshore vendors.

Figure 4: Four-step WORM withdrawal compliance loop resolving the legal tension between permanent optical media and PDPA Section 16 withdrawal rights.

AI Ethics, Minors & Deceased Data Controls

  • 11 Separated Consent Scopes: Inclusion in a family tree or photo archive never implies consent for voice cloning, digital twins, or external AI training (strictly prohibited).
  • Mandatory Synthetic Labeling: All AI-synthesized voice/avatar outputs carry permanent provenance badges to prevent misrepresentation as authentic historical recordings.
  • Age-Tiered Minor Protection: Aligned with PDPC Children's Guidelines (<13 parental consent; 13–17 age-appropriate consent; default prohibition on voice/digital twin synthesis).
  • Deceased Protection (PDPA s.4(4)): Security and disclosure controls maintained for deceased individuals (≤10 years); historical exceptions verified at record level (≥100 years).

Cross-Border & Air-Gapped Data Residency

  • Offshore Access Default-Off: Primary family vault resides physically in Singapore (local Mac mini M5 Pro + Blu-ray); offshore access, telemetry, and external LLM calls are disabled by default.
  • Synthetic-Only R&D: Cross-border engineering teams develop and test exclusively on 100% synthetic/irreversibly anonymized datasets; any exception requires signed ASEAN MCCs and zero-trust timed access.
  • Defamation Safe Harbor (Act 1957): Built-in notice-and-takedown workflow preserves disputed oral history evidence while pausing live access and AI retrieval pending family resolution.
  • SFO Contracting Alignment: Preferential contracting with client SFOs / family holding entities (Pte. Ltd.) to preserve statutory Data Intermediary clarity.
